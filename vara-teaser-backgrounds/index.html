Teaser backgrounds

Animated backgrounds for the VARA Expo 2026 teaser, ready for After Effects. They now run intro to outro: the three YOUR ___ beats, the logo, the five zones, the tagline and the close. No text, logo, tubes or road. Those go on top.

1920×1080 · 30 fps to match the cut · Rec.709 · H.264, high bitrate

Whole film

One plate for the whole film, timed to the 37 s cut. Every wipe sits on that cut’s own transition, so the tube sweeps and road moves cover it. Drop it in at frame 0 and build on top.

Intro → zones → outro → partner cards · 36.9 s · 1107 frames, the whole cut

It opens on the close’s dark ground, so the film starts and ends in the same place. The intro beats swap in 6 frames, under the tubes. The zones wipe in from the right over 26 frames. Summary and the close fall from the top over 32. At the end, the close’s glow rises into a sunrise and floods to the partner cards’ cream (#f4eede). It lands at frame 984, exactly where the cards start, and holds cream to the end.

Using them in After Effects

  1. Quickest: put the whole-film plate in a 1920×1080, 30 fps comp at frame 0, under everything else.
  2. To retime: stack two loops, put that transition’s matte above the incoming one and set it as a Luma Matte, then slide or time-stretch the matte. The loops are seamless, so extend them as far as you need.
  3. Keep the comp at 30 fps. Converting the frame rate will make the drift judder.
