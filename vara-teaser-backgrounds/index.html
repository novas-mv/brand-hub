Using them in After Effects

  1. Quickest: put the full sequence in a 1920×1080, 25 fps comp at frame 0. Titles and icons land on each zone's hold; leave the wipes clear of type.
  2. To retime: stack two zone loops, put that transition's matte above the incoming one and set it as a Luma Matte, then slide or time-stretch the matte. The loops are seamless, so extend them as far as you need.
  3. Keep the comp at 25 fps. Converting frame rates later will make the drift judder.
