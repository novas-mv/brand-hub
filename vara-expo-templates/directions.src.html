VARA Expo 2026 · template exploration · round 3

Two decks and three reports, built out as full template sets

These are the four directions you picked: Daybreak and Night Window for the decks, Annual Review and Night & Day for the report, plus a new Report C that pairs with Daybreak. Each one has every layout a template needs.

  • Real logo. The mark is now the official render from the VARA site, so nothing is clipped or redrawn.
  • Contrast rule. Tubes and glows never cross type. Small coloured labels on cream use darker partners of the zone colours, all at least 4.5:1.
  • Full sets. Each deck has cover, section, agenda, cards, text + image, numbers, table and closing slides. Each report has cover, contents, section opener, narrative, data and back cover pages.

Presentation decks 16:9 · 8 layouts each

DECK B

Daybreak

The night aurora at sunrise. Soft coral, violet and cyan glows bleed in from the edges of a cream page. The logo mark is the hero, and content sits on frosted white panels.

DECK D

Night Window

A light deck with a window onto the night. Slides are cream, and each has one rounded navy panel holding the aurora, tubes or a photo. The divider and closing slides open the window to almost the full frame, which links back to the existing dark deck.

Post-expo report A4 portrait · 6 pages each · sample figures

REPORT A

Annual Review

Calm and editorial. Cream pages, generous margins and two-column text. Tubes only appear on the cover and the section openers. Everywhere else, colour only marks data and pull quotes.

REPORT B

Night & Day

Dark outside, light inside. The cover, section openers and back cover use the night aurora from the existing deck. Reading pages are cream, with a navy rail down the left that shows the section, so you always know where you are.

REPORT C

Daybreak NEW

The report version of Deck B. Pastel aurora on paper, the logo mark as the cover hero, and frosted panels holding the numbers and contents. Photos sit in rounded frames with a soft glow behind them. It's the lightest and friendliest of the three, suited to a public-facing summary.

Tubes are drawn by the Line Shader engine with the brand's own colour ramps. Glows are stand-ins for Weave Gradients renders. The mark is the official render. Type: Hanken Grotesk and JetBrains Mono, both free fonts available in Canva. Round 1 is in this page's version history.
