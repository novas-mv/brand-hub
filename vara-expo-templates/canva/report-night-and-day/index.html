02
The three days
How the floor, the stages and the evening programme ran from Thursday to Saturday.
Doors opened at 16:00 on Thursday 26 November to queues along the Central Park promenade. By the first evening the Career zone had taken more walk-in interviews than on any day of the 2025 edition.
The Skills bays ran live welding, marine engineering and culinary competitions on all three days, with heats on Friday and finals on the Saturday evening stage.
Education sessions ran twice daily in Dhivehi and English. The livestreamed panels drew a peak online audience on Friday night.
“The best-attended skills event we've run in the Maldives.”
